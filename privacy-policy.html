Privacy Policy

Body Awareness Pilates Planner · Last updated: August 8, 2026

Overview

Body Awareness Pilates Planner ("the App") is a planning and practice-management tool for Pilates instructors and certification students. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Information We Collect

Account information you provide:

  • Name, email address, mobile number
  • Business information (business name, phone, email, address) — optional
  • A profile photo — optional

Content you create:

  • Exercises, classes, and class plans you build
  • Client notes, session history, and homework assignments (if you use these features)
  • Continuing education records, certifications, and practice logs
  • Submissions and feedback exchanged within Teacher Trainer student groups

All of the above is stored under your own individual account and is private to you by default. It is never visible to other users unless you take an explicit action to share it (see "Sharing" below).

How Information Is Stored

The App uses Supabase (a third-party backend service) to store account data and files. Supabase processes this data on our behalf, under access controls that restrict each account's data to that account alone. We do not sell or rent your information to any third party.

Sharing

Your content is never shared automatically. It is only shared when you take a deliberate action to do so, such as:

  • Share with Code — generates a one-time code that lets another user copy an exercise or class into their own library. This creates an independent copy; it is not a live link, and edits you make afterward do not affect a copy someone else has already made.
  • Submit to Group (students) — sends a class or exercise to the instructor(s) of a group you belong to, for review and feedback.
  • Send to Group (Teacher Trainers) — sends a class, exercise, or homework assignment to specific students or all students in a group you run.
  • Export to PDF — generates a document you choose to share outside the App (e.g. with a client who doesn't use the App).

Crash Reporting

The App uses Sentry, a third-party crash-reporting service, to help us detect and fix technical problems. Sentry automatically collects, when a technical error occurs:

  • Error messages and stack traces
  • Device and app information (operating system, device model, app version)
  • A short trail of recent in-app actions leading up to the error

This information is collected anonymously. We do not send your name, email address, or IP address to Sentry, and crash reports are not linked to your identity or account. Network request details are filtered to remove any sensitive information before being logged.

The App does not use any other analytics, advertising, or user-tracking services.

Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete most of your own content directly within the App.
  • You can remove your profile photo at any time.
  • Account deletion: to be completed — contact us at the email below to request account deletion in the meantime.

Children's Privacy

This App is intended for use by Pilates professionals, instructors, and certification students. It is not directed at, marketed to, or knowingly used by children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date above.
