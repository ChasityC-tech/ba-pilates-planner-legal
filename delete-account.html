The Pilates Planner by Body Awareness · Last updated: August 13, 2026

How to Delete Your Account

Account deletion is available directly in the App — no need to contact us:

  1. Sign in to The Pilates Planner
  2. Open Settings (gear icon in the top right of any screen)
  3. Tap Delete My Account
  4. Type DELETE to confirm

Deletion happens immediately — there is no waiting period.

What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account, login, and profile information (name, contact details, profile photo, business info)
  • Your exercise library, classes, and class plans
  • Your client records, session history, and homework assignments
  • Your continuing education records, certifications, and practice logs
  • Any uploaded files (profile photo, business logo, certificates)

What's Retained, and Why

If you've submitted a class, exercise, or homework assignment to a Teacher Trainer group, or received feedback within one, that content lives in other members' history — not just your own. Deleting your account does not remove those records, since doing so would also erase other people's data. Instead, your identity on those specific records is anonymized: your name is replaced with "Former Student" or "Former Trainer," and no other personal information about you remains attached to them.
